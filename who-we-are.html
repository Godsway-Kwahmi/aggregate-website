Who We Are

Established and registered in 2016 By Mr. Adolph Kwabla Amevor. Aggregate Construction, a sole proprietorship under business names act 1962 (Act 151).

We are always available with the necessary skills, knowledge and equipment to meet any requirement in the field of general construction, civil construction, electrical and plumbing installations.

Safe working conditions are a very high priority to us not only for our employees, but also for our customers. This not only enables us to deliver a dynamic and professional service to our customers, but also gives the client the assurance that their interest is served by members and staff personally to complete projects on time and to the required quality standards.

Our experience and expertise as building contractors mean we can handle even the largest project with complete confidence, we are also equally adept at handling the smallest project. However, no matter how big or small the project, our customers are rest assured that it will deliver on time, within their budget and to the satisfaction of our clients.
